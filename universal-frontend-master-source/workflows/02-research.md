# 02 — Research

## Goal
Research requirements, references, design inspiration, components, templates, fonts, icons,
images, and videos. Verify licenses and commercial-use permissions.

## Steps
1. Read `.ufme/PROJECT_SPEC.md` and any reference website/screenshot.
2. Identify needed assets (fonts, icons, images, templates, components).
3. For each asset, verify license and commercial-use status.
4. Record asset decisions (name, repo/site, license, commercial-use, install method, reason)
   in `.ufme/DECISIONS.md` via the orchestrator.
5. Update `.ufme/TASKS.md` (T-03) and `.ufme/AGENT_STATE.md`.
6. Delegate next: backend intelligence (if backend) or product intelligence.

## Output
- Research notes
- Asset decisions in `.ufme/DECISIONS.md`
- `.ufme/TASKS.md` (T-03 COMPLETE)
