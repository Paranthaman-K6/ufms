# ufmw seo verify <url>

Re-crawl the website after fixes and verify that SEO issues have been
resolved. Compares before/after crawl data.

Usage:
  ufmw seo verify <url>

Example:
  ufmw seo verify https://example.com

Output:
  - .ufme/evidence/seo-verify.json — verification results
  - .ufme/SEO_RESULTS.md — human-readable results
