# ufmw seo plan <url>

Generate a prioritized SEO improvement plan ranking issues by
severity, effort, and impact.

Usage:
  ufmw seo plan <url>

Example:
  ufmw seo plan https://example.com

Output:
  - .ufme/evidence/seo-plan.json — machine-readable plan
  - .ufme/SEO_PLAN.md — human-readable plan
