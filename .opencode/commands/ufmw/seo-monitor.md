# ufmw seo monitor <url>

Monitor SEO metrics over time by recording crawl results and comparing
against previous measurements.

Usage:
  ufmw seo monitor <url>

Example:
  ufmw seo monitor https://example.com

Output:
  - .ufme/SEO_METRICS.md — historical metrics
  - .ufme/evidence/seo-metrics.json — machine-readable metrics
